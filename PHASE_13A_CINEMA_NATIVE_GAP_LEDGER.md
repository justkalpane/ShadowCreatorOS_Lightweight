## Drift ledger

The dominant drift patterns are:

- `YOUTUBE_CONTENT_DRIFT`
- `GENERIC_SCRIPT_DRIFT`
- `VISUAL_MEDIA_DRIFT`
- `RUNTIME_PROOF_DRIFT`
- `MISSING_FILMCRAFT_GATE`

These drift types are not failures by themselves. They are the reason the alignment verdict remains partial rather than complete.

## Patch priorities

1. Establish explicit cinema-story architecture ownership.
2. Separate screenplay logic from content scripting.
3. Promote scene, sequence, dialogue, and visual grammar into a cinema-native core.
4. Add film style, docudrama ethics, and source-vs-render separation as first-class gates.
5. Keep runtime proof blocked until the cinema brain alignment is actually implemented and verified.
