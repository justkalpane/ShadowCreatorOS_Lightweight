## 3. Downstream and governance note
Distribution and runtime proof are necessary departments, but they are not creative screenplay authorship. Craft 23 is downstream only. Craft 24 is governance/proof only. Neither may control the cinema core.

## 4. Canon rule
The canon is a target operating model, not a claim that every existing repo surface already behaves this way.
