## 5. Architecture intent
The production-house model must treat cinema as the primary creative system and content packaging as a downstream service. The film route should own screenplay, scene, character, visual, sound, and craft logic. Content repurposing, thumbnails, shorts, hooks, and platform metadata belong outside the cinema core.

## 6. Roll-forward rule
Future implementation phases must move in waves, not by random broad rewrites. Each wave should carry its own inventory, validation gate, and rollback rule.
